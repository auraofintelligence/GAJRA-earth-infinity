## Reinterpret

- Global synchronisation becomes a gradual convergence pathway, not an announced event.
- World voting becomes plural preference comparison, justification and disagreement-preserving research.
- Gamification becomes voluntary lived experiments without manipulative engagement scoring.
- Digital twins become challengeable models and rehearsal tools, not digital masters.
- Alignment data becomes raw, provenance-rich contribution material rather than an automatic training dataset.

## Visual and media decisions

- NASA SVS item 31375 is the preferred aurora source. The source page lists NASA Johnson Space Center imagery and a 2026 NASA SVS release.
- NASA must be credited and no endorsement implied.
- Historic 1967 flower-and-rifle photographs will not be copied. Any flower/force image must be rights-safe original work with a distinct composition.
- Major hero art will use raster, video, CSS or canvas rather than a flat SVG solar-system illustration.
